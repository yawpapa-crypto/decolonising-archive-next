## Integrity rule
Queued rows are research targets, not claims that an object, maker, date or collection has already been verified.
A queued record should move to `published` only after:
1. one authoritative primary source or two independent credible sources;
2. creator, date, locality and object type are checked;
3. rights and community authority are documented;
4. provenance and colonial acquisition history are recorded where relevant;
5. source URLs and verification notes are complete.

## Catalogue principle
The collection begins with Ghanaian systems of visual communication, authority, memory,
identity, public instruction and everyday making. It does not begin with European print
or the modern professional design studio.
